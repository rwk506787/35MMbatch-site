What to include

  • What you were trying to do.
  • Your iPhone model and iOS version.
  • Whether the issue happened during import, preview, develop or export.
  • The diagnostics text from About & Help, if you are comfortable sharing it.

Instant frames and photo shape

Select Instant in the editor for retro colour and an automatic white border with a deeper bottom margin. Choose Original to keep the image shape, or Square fit to keep the whole image inside a square area without cropping. These controls follow the editor's All or This photo selection.

Editing originals in Apple Photos

Edit originals in Photos applies the finished look and frame to the same still-photo item as a JPEG edit. You can undo it using Photos' Revert action. Choose Original size to retain the photo pixels; the frame increases the canvas size. Live Photos need a still-photo copy instead, leaving the original Live Photo and its motion unchanged.

35mm Retro processes photos on your device. Please do not email photos or diagnostics unless you want support to receive them.
