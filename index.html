Made with an analogue heart

Built for the holiday reel, the camera roll and everything in between.

35mm Retro is a vintage photo editor inspired by analogue film. It was made by Russ, who loves shooting 35mm film and wanted a quicker way to bring that feeling to digital photos in bulk. The looks are tuned using his own photographs and favourite film stocks.

Good to know

Your photos. A little film character.

Can I edit photos already on my iPhone?

Yes. Import existing photos, choose a film look and apply it to a whole batch. You can compare each preview with its original before developing.

What is free?

Your first 36 successfully developed photos include all three film looks and the strength and grain controls. After that, optional Unlimited access removes the allowance limit through a weekly or annual subscription, or a one-time lifetime purchase.

Can I keep the original size?

Yes. Choose Original size when saving new copies, exporting files or sharing. JPG and PNG are supported. Editing an original in Photos uses a separate output path; use copies or file exports to preserve the original pixel dimensions.

Do I need an account?

No. Your photos are processed on your iPhone. Purchases are handled by Apple.

Is this the app previously called 35mm Batch?

Yes. 35mm Retro is the same app with a clearer name. Existing purchases and qualifying early-user Unlimited access continue to work.
