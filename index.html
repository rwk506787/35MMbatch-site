Made with an analogue heart

Built for the holiday reel, the camera roll and everything in between.

35mm Retro is a vintage photo editor inspired by analogue film. It was made by Russ, who loves shooting 35mm film and wanted a quicker way to bring that feeling to digital photos in bulk. The looks are tuned using his own photographs and favourite film stocks.

Good to know

Your photos. A little film character.

Can I edit photos already on my iPhone?

Yes. Import existing photos, choose one film look for the batch or different looks for each photo. You can compare each preview with its original before developing.

What is free?

Your first 36 successfully developed photos include all four film looks, strength and grain controls, and Instant framing. After that, optional Unlimited access removes the allowance limit through a weekly or annual subscription, or a one-time lifetime purchase.

Can I keep the original size?

Yes. Choose Original size to retain the photo pixels. A frame adds extra canvas around them. Copies and file exports support JPG and PNG; reversible edits to existing still photos in Apple Photos use JPEG. Live Photos must be saved as still-photo copies so their original motion stays intact.

Can I add an instant-photo border?

Choose Instant in the editor for an automatic white frame with a deeper bottom margin. Use Original or Square fit below Grain to keep the original photo shape or fit the whole photo inside a square image area, without cropping or stretching it.

Do I need an account?

No. Your photos are processed on your iPhone. Purchases are handled by Apple.

Is this the app previously called 35mm Batch?

Yes. 35mm Retro is the same app with a clearer name. Existing purchases and qualifying early-user Unlimited access continue to work.
